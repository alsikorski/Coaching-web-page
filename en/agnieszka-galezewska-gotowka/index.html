Growth on your own terms

Some situations can occupy our thoughts for a long time, yet we keep arriving at the same conclusions. They may concern work, relationships or an important decision. Sometimes it is hard even to name what needs to change. I enjoy working with precisely these less obvious questions. I create space to pause, reflect and discover possibilities that may not yet be visible.

How I work

I do not assume where our conversation should lead. I am interested in what lies beneath the first answer or a familiar pattern of behaviour. I do not make decisions for clients; I help them understand their situation and choose for themselves. Agency does not always mean doing more. Coaching is not a substitute for diagnosis, psychotherapy or medical treatment.

My experience

I spent over a decade in fast-paced business environments in management and training roles. I led teams and operations and developed people and managers. I completed postgraduate studies in Professional Coaching – Methods and Practice at Kozminski University, its Business Trainers School, and the Meritum School of Coaches, earning the Meritum Certified Coach title.
