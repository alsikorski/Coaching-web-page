03 / How I work

How I work

I combine genuine attention to the person with a practical mindset. In coaching, I do not provide ready-made answers: I help you explore possibilities and develop your own solutions. In mentoring, I also share industry experience and practical insights. ADHD coaching is not a substitute for assessment, psychotherapy or medical treatment.

04 / My experience

My experience

I have worked for many years in IT and international organisations, across project management, PMO, Agile, Scrum and Data & AI. I have worked with technology teams, specialists and leaders. I completed postgraduate studies in Professional Coaching at Kozminski University.
