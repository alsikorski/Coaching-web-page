Growth on your own terms

Good management is not about having an answer to every question. It is about building teams, processes and ways of working that help people respond effectively to everyday challenges. I have spent nearly 20 years in manufacturing, managing teams ranging from dozens to hundreds of people. Today I bring that experience to coaching, mentoring, training and consulting.

Management coaching and mentoring

How I work

My approach is concrete, structured and practical. Through coaching I support leaders in building autonomy and accountability. Through mentoring I share management experience. In consulting and workshops, I use exercises, case studies and participants’ real challenges. Tools should serve people and business needs, not become an end in themselves.

My experience

I have nearly 20 years of experience in manufacturing, operations management, leadership development and process improvement. I have managed teams of dozens to hundreds, led LEAN projects, designed KPI systems and delivered training in leadership, communication, change, problem solving and visual management.
