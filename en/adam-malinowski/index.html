03 / How I work

How I work

My approach is concrete, structured and practical. Through coaching I support leaders in building autonomy and accountability. Through mentoring I share management experience. In consulting and workshops, I use exercises, case studies and participants’ real challenges. Tools should serve people and business needs, not become an end in themselves.
